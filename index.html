• HERITAGE •

Time really matters

Discover the captivating journey of Olympic Exports Pvt. Ltd., ignited in 1994 in the historic leather hub of Jajmau, Kanpur. Across generations, our founding ethos of uncompromising excellence lives on through Managing Director Farhan Mirza, Director Rukhsaar Mirza, and Director Ayaan Mirza, shaping Olympic Exports into a Govt. Recognized Export House.

From master vegetable-tanned grain selection to intricate hand-incised floral tooling, handwoven Aztec beadwork, Southwestern Kilim luggage, and technical narrow fabrics, our dedication to efficiency, commercial integrity, and European REACH compliance defines our evolution from a heritage atelier into an international export powerhouse.
