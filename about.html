Tracing the Roots of Olympic Exports

Woven With Excellence

The story of Olympic Exports Pvt. Ltd. is one of quiet, relentless craftsmanship — born in the historic leather quarter of Jajmau, Kanpur in 1994. From our foundational workshop on Defence Colony, we have grown into a premier Govt. Recognized Export House, supplying luxury leather goods and technical components to buyers across the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, and Australia.

Under the active stewardship of Managing Director Farhan Mirza, Director Rukhsaar Mirza, and Director Ayaan Mirza, we have maintained an uncompromising benchmark across all 7 manufacturing divisions — from hand-carved Western tooling belts and tribal Aztec beaded straps to handwoven Kilim tapestries, technical narrow fabrics, and custom footwear cordage.

Our location in Jajmau — India's premier leather processing epicenter — provides direct access to premium vegetable-tanned hides, generational tooling artisans, and the operational agility of a family-run atelier with full industrial export scale.

Chronology of Growth

Three Decades of Industrial Excellence

1994

Establishment in Jajmau, Kanpur

Founded on Defence Colony as an artisanal manufacturing workshop dedicated to Western leather tooling belts, shoelaces, and saddlery hardware.

2003

Expansion into Narrow Fabrics & Elastics

Commissioned high-speed needle looms and braided cordage machinery to supply technical boot gussets, apparel waistbands, and glazed waxed cords.

2012

Tribal Beaded Inlays & Kilim Tapestry Bags

Integrated generational handloom weavers and bead artisans, launching our iconic Southwestern Kilim luggage and Aztec beaded belts export lines.

2020

High-Speed Needle Looms & Technical Elastics

Installed computer-controlled narrow-fabric looms and silicone-coating machinery to manufacture high-tensile technical webbings and jacquard elastics.

Present

Global B2B Export House

Serving buying houses and boutique brands in 35+ countries with private label tooling dies, rapid 48-hour sample dispatches, and full REACH compliance.
